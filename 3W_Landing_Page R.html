What We Offer

Six Core Service Lines

End-to-end aviation support — from aircraft inspections and fleet management to engine technical oversight and cargo logistics.

✈️

Aircraft & Fleet Management

Commercial negotiation, on-site inspection, and complete fleet lifecycle support from phase-in through end-of-lease.

  • Annual audit & end-of-lease inspections
  • Aircraft records validation & preparation
  • Fleet phase-in / phase-out advisory
  • Return-to-lessor documentation
  • Aircraft sourcing: purchase, lease & charter
🛠️

Fleet Technical Management (FTM)

Comprehensive, cost-effective engineering and maintenance solutions designed for both full-service airlines and low-fare operators.

  • Customised maintenance program design
  • Maintenance department set-up
  • On-site representation at MRO facilities
  • QA & regulatory authority compliance
  • Airworthiness requirement management
📋

CAMO Service Delivery

Continuing Airworthiness Management — monitoring, recording, and controlling all maintenance requirements to keep your fleet compliant.

  • Aircraft operating data monitoring & recording
  • Approved maintenance data management
  • Life limited component (LLC) administration
  • AD / SB compliance statements
  • Periodic technical & reliability reviews
⚙️

Engine Technical Management

Maximise engine availability and minimise costs through proactive monitoring, LLP management, and optimised shop visit planning.

  • LLP management & planned removals
  • Engine utilisation monitoring
  • Shop visit scheduling & workscope prep
  • On-wing & NSV AD/SB tracking
  • Component maintenance cost optimisation
📦

Spare Parts Supply Chain

Reliable sourcing and distribution of spare parts across the full aircraft spectrum — from narrow-body jets to widebody aircraft.

  • Parts sourcing for commercial jets & widebodies
  • Supply chain collaboration & arrangement
  • Regulatory-compliant parts provisioning
  • Inventory investment optimisation
  • Dispatch schedule risk management
🌏

Cargo Charter Flights

Flexible air cargo transportation solutions with full in-country administrative support across ASEAN and East Asia.

  • Cargo charter sourcing & coordination
  • In-country administrative support
  • ASEAN & East Asia facilitation
  • Tailored logistics to client requirements
  • End-to-end cargo flight management
